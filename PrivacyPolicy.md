# Privacy Policy for PianoSync

**Effective Date:** May 29, 2025  
**Last Updated:** May 29, 2025

## Overview

PianoSync is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our piano learning application.

## The Simple Truth

**PianoSync collects absolutely no personal data.**

## What We Don't Collect

- **No Personal Information**: We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers
- **No Usage Analytics**: We don't track how you use the app, which features you access, or how often you practice
- **No Device Information**: We don't collect device IDs, operating system details, or hardware specifications
- **No Location Data**: We don't access or store your location information
- **No Audio Recordings**: While the app processes MIDI input for piano practice, no audio is recorded or stored
- **No Practice Data**: Your practice sessions, progress, and performance metrics remain entirely on your device

## Data Storage

All app data, including:
- Your practice progress
- Song preferences
- App settings
- Performance history

This information is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Internet Connectivity

PianoSync is designed to work completely offline. The app:
- **Does not require an internet connection** to function
- **Does not connect to external servers**
- **Does not send or receive any data over the internet**
- **Does not contain advertisements** that might track users

## Open Source Transparency

PianoSync is an open-source application. This means:
- The complete source code is publicly available for review
- Anyone can verify that we don't collect data by examining the code
- The community can contribute to and audit the app's privacy practices
- You can view our code repository to see exactly what the app does

## Third-Party Services

PianoSync does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs that could collect your data.

## Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data whatsoever, PianoSync is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. No personal information is collected from anyone, regardless of age.

## Data Security

While we don't collect any data, the information stored locally on your device (such as your practice progress) is protected by your device's built-in security features. We recommend:
- Keeping your device updated with the latest security patches
- Using device lock screens and encryption features provided by your operating system

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If our privacy practices ever change (which is unlikely given our no-data-collection approach), we will update this policy and notify users through:
- App updates
- Our project repository
- Our official communication channels

## Your Rights

Since we collect no data, there is:
- No data to access, correct, or delete
- No data to export or transfer
- No data processing to object to
- No automated decision-making based on your data

## Transparency Commitment

As an open-source project, we believe in complete transparency. You can:
- Review our source code to verify these privacy claims
- Contribute to the project and help maintain these privacy standards
- Report any privacy concerns through our official channels

---

**Summary**: PianoSync is a privacy-first piano learning app that operates entirely offline, collects no personal data, and stores all information locally on your device. Your practice sessions are yours alone.
